# PAYGUARD — War Room Demo

The War Room is a presentation layer over the deterministic 100-transaction adversarial benchmark.

## What it demonstrates

- Progressive incident discovery
- Autonomous vs human vs blocked decisions
- Verified recovery accumulation
- Deterministic, reproducible benchmark data
- Explicit disclosure that all amounts are simulated

## Demo sequence

1. Open the merchant dashboard.
2. Click **Run incident sweep**.
3. Let the War Room replay the incident storm.
4. Highlight the mix of autonomous recoveries, human approvals, and blocked actions.
5. Open a blocked high-risk incident and show the policy decision.
6. Open the Decision Ledger and show the audit trail.

## Important disclosure

The replay is simulated and does not move real merchant money. The Razorpay adapter is read/verify-only in the MVP.
